# FS-115 · Orchestrator schedule, catchup, timeout or trigger semantics changed without re-deriving dependents

Airflow DAG cadence, catchup, start_date, execution_timeout, waiter attempts, trigger rules, TaskGroup factories and XCom flows are edited in isolation: lookback and day-of-month logic still assume the old cadence, catchup replays from a stale start_date, timeouts are shorter than the external job and retries start a second writer, fixed task_ids collide in factories, wall-clock values are recomputed across an approval gate, and recovery advice relies on XCom from a hand-marked task.

**Triggers**
- changing schedule_interval/cron, catchup or start_date
- adding an EMR/Batch operator or sensor with waiter_max_attempts or execution_timeout
- a fan-in task after .expand(), or a TaskGroup factory called per platform
- tasks that each compute dates from now(), or a recovery plan that marks tasks success

**Guards**
- Grep the DAG for every value derived from the schedule (lookback, day-of-month, data_interval) and update them together
- Size timeouts and waiter attempts x delay to the measured p99 job runtime; confirm retries terminate the underlying job
- When enabling catchup, move start_date to the deploy date
- Compute time-derived values once and pass them via XCom/params; ensure downstream tasks tolerate a hand-marked upstream
