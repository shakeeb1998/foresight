# FS-120 · Cost or rate metric computed with the wrong denominator, tier or components

Per-unit costs and rates are derived from totals that include unrelated spend (evaluator cost), divided by a proxy count, labelled with a pricing tier the formula doesn't apply, or copy a per-request aggregate onto every exploded row. The number looks reasonable and drives a decision.

**Triggers**
- estimating prod cost from a benchmark or bill
- a column name that claims a tier/unit the formula must honor
- exploding a batched response that carries a per-request usage field

**Guards**
- Write the formula with numerator and denominator sources named, and recompute from stored inputs
- Exclude benchmark-only components from prod extrapolations
- Divide per-request aggregates across exploded rows or keep them at request grain
